# Reverse lunges with pulse

**Target muscles:** Glutes, quadriceps, hamstrings.

## Starting position

Stand upright, feet hip-width apart, hands on hips (later you can hold light dumbbells at your sides). Torso vertical, chest open.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Step one leg back and lower: the front thigh nearly parallel to the floor, the rear knee near the floor, the torso leaning slightly forward (10–15°).
2. At the bottom, do 2–3 small pulses of 5–10 cm, staying in the lower part of the range.
3. Push up through the heel of the front foot and return to the start. Work 20 seconds on each leg without a break between them (40 seconds total).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

A reverse lunge is easier on the knees and spine than a forward lunge. A slight torso lean (10–15°) takes stress off the lower back with hyperlordosis. The front knee does not cave in or travel far past the toes. Do not arch the lower back at the bottom.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Only a mat is needed. If balance is hard, hold the back of a chair with your free hand.
