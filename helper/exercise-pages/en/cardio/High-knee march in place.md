# High-knee march in place

**Target muscles:** Hip flexors, quadriceps, glutes, cardiovascular system.

## Starting position

Stand upright, feet hip-width apart, arms bent at the elbows by your sides. Torso vertical, neck neutral, eyes forward.

## Step-by-step technique

1. March in place, raising the knee to hip level; the arms swing opposite the legs, as in walking.
2. Land on the ball of the foot softly, without slapping. The pace is one where you can still speak in short phrases.
3. Keep the abs slightly braced, breathe evenly, shoulders down.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No jumping, so impact on the spine is minimal: this exercise can replace the jump rope when your neck or back hurts. Do not lean back as you raise the knee and do not arch the lower back with hyperlordosis. Head level, chin not lifted.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You only need a free spot on the floor. If balance wobbles, put a chair nearby and hold the backrest.
