# Mountain climbers

**Target muscles:** Core and abs, front delts, hip flexors; high heart rate.

## Starting position

Get into a high plank: palms under the shoulders, arms straight, body one line from head to heels. Abs and glutes braced, pelvis slightly tucked to remove the arch in the lower back.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Drive one knee toward the chest without lifting the hips; immediately return the leg and drive the other.
2. Alternate legs at a fast but controlled pace for 40 seconds, breathing steadily, shoulders over the palms.
3. If your form falls apart (hips bounce up or sag), slow the pace down but do not stop.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The main risk with hyperlordosis is the lower back sagging: keep the pelvis slightly tucked and the abs braced. Do not rock the hips up and down or twist the torso. The head continues the spine, eyes on the floor between the palms. If your lower back hurts, slow down or put your palms on a chair seat.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You need a mat. To make it easier, place your palms on the seat of a stable chair or sofa: an incline plank is easier on the lower back and shoulders.
