# Jump rope: Boxer Skip

**Target muscles:** Calves, ankles, coordination; cardiovascular system.

## Starting position

Stand upright with the rope behind you, elbows at your sides, hands at hip level. Weight on the balls of your feet, knees soft.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Turn the rope with your wrists and skip over it, shifting weight from foot to foot: one foot lands on each turn, like boxing footwork.
2. Jump low (1–2 cm) and softly, on the ball of the foot; the heels do not touch the floor.
3. Keep a steady rhythm and breathing for all 40 seconds: torso vertical, eyes forward.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Land softly and do not jump high: the impact falls on one leg at a time. Use a mat or springy surface and cushioned shoes. Torso vertical, lower back not arched, head not tilted back. If your back or neck hurts, replace it with stepping in place or light boxer footwork without jumping.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You need a jump rope. If the step is hard to get, first just shift your weight from foot to foot without a rope, then add turning the rope with the wrists.
