# Plank shoulder taps

**Target muscles:** Core muscles (transverse abdominis and obliques), shoulders, scapular stabilizers.

## Starting position

Get into a high plank with the feet set wide (wider than shoulders). Palms under the shoulders; abs, glutes and quads braced; body in a straight line.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Without changing the pelvis position, lift one palm off the floor and touch the opposite shoulder with it.
2. Put the hand back on the floor and repeat with the other hand; alternate at a slow, controlled pace.
3. Make sure the pelvis does not rock side to side: imagine a glass of water on your lower back. Work for 40 seconds.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The wide foot stance reduces torso rotation, which matters in scoliosis. Do not arch the lower back: keep the abs and glutes braced. Head in line with the spine, eyes on the floor. If your wrists or lower back hurt, do it with your hands on a chair seat or from the knees.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Only a mat is needed. To make it easier, put your palms on a chair seat.
