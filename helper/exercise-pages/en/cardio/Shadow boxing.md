# Shadow boxing

**Target muscles:** Shoulders, triceps, core muscles, legs; cardiovascular system.

## Starting position

Stand in a light stance: feet slightly wider than shoulders, one foot slightly forward, knees soft, fists by the chin, elbows tucked to the body.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Alternate straight punches (jab and cross), turning the hip and foot with each punch. The rotation comes from the hips and feet, not the lower back.
2. Add light slips and move 1–2 steps, staying on the balls of the feet.
3. Work at a moderate pace for all 90 seconds, breathing evenly: exhale on every punch.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not lock out the elbow at the end of the punch: the arm stays "soft". Do not twist the lower back (scoliosis) and do not lean back. Chin slightly lowered, neck neutral.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You need about 2 × 2 m of free space. Start with no weights in your hands.
