# Jump rope: Low Bounce

**Target muscles:** Calves, feet and ankles, cardiovascular system.

## Starting position

Stand upright with the rope behind you, handles in your hands at hip level. Elbows pinned to your sides, eyes forward, neck neutral. Feet together or slightly apart, knees soft.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Turn the rope with your wrists, not the whole arm: the elbows are almost still, forearms slightly out to the sides.
2. Jump low: 1–2 cm off the floor, landing on the ball of the foot with soft knees and springy ankles.
3. Keep a steady rhythm and breathing for all 40 seconds: torso vertical, abs slightly braced, shoulders down.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Jumping puts impact load on the spine, so keep jumps low and soft, on a mat or springy floor and in cushioned shoes (not on tile or concrete). Do not tilt the head back or lift the chin (neck) and do not arch the lower back with hyperlordosis. If your neck or back hurts, swap the rope for marching in place with high knees or a no-jump step-jack.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You need a jump rope. The length is right if the handles reach your armpits when you stand on the middle of the rope. If space is tight or you have no rope, mimic turning the rope with your wrists without one.
