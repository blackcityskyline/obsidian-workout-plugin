# Bear plank

**Target muscles:** Quadriceps, core muscles, shoulders.

## Starting position

Get on all fours on a mat: palms under the shoulders, knees under the hips, feet on the toes. Back flat, neck continues the spine, eyes on the floor.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, brace the abs and quads and lift the knees 2–3 cm off the floor; the back stays parallel to the floor.
2. Hold the position for 40 seconds, breathing evenly without holding your breath; hips no higher than the shoulders.
3. To make it harder, add micro-steps: alternately lift and place the palms or feet by 1–2 cm without rocking the pelvis.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

With hyperlordosis make sure the lower back does not arch: slightly tuck the pelvis and keep the abs braced. Do not raise the hips above shoulder line or let them sag. If your lower back hurts or your form falls apart, lower the knees to the floor, rest, then continue. Do not tilt the head back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You need a mat. To make it harder, hold longer or add micro-steps.
