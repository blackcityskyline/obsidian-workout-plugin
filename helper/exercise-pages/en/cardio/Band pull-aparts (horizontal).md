# Band pull-aparts (horizontal)

**Target muscles:** Rear delts, rhomboids, middle trapezius.

## Starting position

Stand upright and hold a band (loop or tube) with an overhand grip shoulder-width apart. Arms are straight out in front at chest level, elbows slightly bent, shoulder blades down.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, pull the arms apart to the sides, stretching the band to shoulder line or until it touches the chest, squeezing the shoulder blades.
2. Hold for 1 second at the end point without shrugging toward the ears.
3. On the inhale, return the arms forward under control (2–3 seconds). Breathe evenly: this exercise helps you recover your breath after hard movements.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Feet shoulder-width apart, torso still: do not arch the lower back or push the head forward. Chin slightly tucked, neck long. Good for correcting slouching and kyphosis; with cervical osteochondrosis do not tilt the head back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You need a medium-resistance band. The narrower the grip, the harder it is: start with a wide one.
