# Band ski-pole pull-downs

**Target muscles:** Lats, rear delts, triceps, core muscles.

## Starting position

Anchor a long resistance band to a door handle (or a door anchor) at head height or above. Stand facing the door 1–1.5 m away and take the band ends in both hands. Feet shoulder-width apart, knees soft, a slight forward lean from the hips, back straight.

## Step-by-step technique

1. From the position with arms stretched forward and up, pull the band down and back toward the hips on the exhale, as if pushing off with ski poles; the shoulder blades squeeze together.
2. At the same time, squat slightly and push the hips back, accelerating the movement. Work in a fast, explosive tempo for all 40 seconds.
3. Return the arms forward and up under control, not letting the band snap your arms back.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The lean comes from the hip joints and the back stays straight: do not round the upper or lower back and do not arch backward when the arms go up. Do not shrug toward the ears or push the head forward (neck). Choose band resistance at which the torso stays still and you are not pulled forward.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You need a long band with handles or a loop band and a door anchor (or a sturdy door handle). The door should open away from you: then the tension presses it against the frame and it will not swing open.
