# Lateral band walks

**Target muscles:** Gluteus medius, hip abductors.

## Starting position

Put a mini loop band above the knees (later, above the ankles for more load). Stand in a light half squat: feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent, torso leaning forward 10–15°, hands in front of the chest.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Take a step to the side, spreading the legs and not letting the band pull the knees inward.
2. Bring the other foot in so the distance between the feet stays about shoulder-width and the band is always taut.
3. Walk 20 seconds in one direction, then 20 seconds in the other (40 seconds total), without standing up.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the pelvis level and do not sway the torso side to side: this matters in scoliosis so that the glutes, not the lower back, take the load. Knees point the same way as the toes and do not cave in. Back straight.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You need a mini loop band (a fabric one does not slide on your thighs). If the room is tight, step back and forth 3–4 steps.
