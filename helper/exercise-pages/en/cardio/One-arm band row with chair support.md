# One-arm band row with chair support

**Target muscles:** Lats, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps.

## Starting position

Put a stable chair against a wall. Rest your left hand and left knee on the seat (torso parallel to the floor, back flat). Your right foot stands on the middle of the band to pin it; the right hand holds the band end.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, pull the band in an arc toward the hip (the "pocket"), keeping the elbow close to the body.
2. At the top, squeeze the shoulder blade toward the spine and hold for 1 second.
3. On the inhale, return the arm down under control (2–3 seconds). Work 20 seconds on each arm (40 seconds total).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Three points of support fully unload the lower back, as in the dumbbell row supported on the sofa. Do not twist the torso or yank; back flat, neck continues the spine, eyes on the floor.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Any stable chair pushed against a wall will do. Pick a band so the last reps take effort while your form does not break down.
