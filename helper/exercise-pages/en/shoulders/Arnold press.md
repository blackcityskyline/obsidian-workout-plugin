# Arnold press

**Better still** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Front delts

## Starting position

Sit with the dumbbells in front of your chest, palms facing you.

## Step-by-step technique

1. As you begin pressing up, rotate your forearms outward.
2. At the top, your palms face forward.
3. As you lower, reverse the rotation.

## Key points

- Works the front and side delts.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating too fast right at the start of the movement.
