# Dumbbell scoop press

**Almost best** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Front delts

## Starting position

Hold the dumbbells in front of you with a neutral grip.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press the dumbbells up and slightly forward in an arc, as if you were "scooping" air with your elbows.

## Key points

- Keeps the front delt constantly active with no loss of tension.

## Common mistakes

- Drifting the dumbbells far back behind your head.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Overhead pressing can arch the lower back. Press in an arc without leaning back, ribs down and glutes tight; sit against a high chair back if the back arches.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Seated on a chair with a high back and dumbbells of up to 16 kg.
