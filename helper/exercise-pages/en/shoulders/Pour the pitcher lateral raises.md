# Pour the pitcher lateral raises

**Worst** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Side delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Raise the dumbbells to the sides with the pinkies up, as if pouring a pitcher.

## Key points

- Stop pouring the pitcher: turn the thumbs up and a bad shoulder exercise becomes a great one.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No special back risk, but the pinkies-up grip internally rotates the shoulder. Turn the thumbs up.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do the normal lateral raise with dumbbells up to 16 kg and thumbs up.
