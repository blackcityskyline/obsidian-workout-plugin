# Dumbbell cheat laterals

**Better** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Side delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Swing the dumbbells up to the sides with a small hip drive, then lower them slowly.

## Key points

- A good middle delt builder because it lets you load heavier and place an eccentric overload, but other muscles substitute.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the body swing small and the abs braced; a big heave becomes a lumbar arch.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells up to 16 kg.
