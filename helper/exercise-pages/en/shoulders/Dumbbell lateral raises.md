# Dumbbell lateral raises

**Almost best** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Side delts (shoulder width).

## Starting position

Stand upright, dumbbells in front of you, elbows slightly bent.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, raise the arms out to the sides up to shoulder level (no higher!). Lead with the elbows.
2. Pause for 1 second at the top.
3. On the inhale, lower the arms under control.

## Key points

- Isolates the side delts.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the weight up with momentum (cheating) and raising your hands higher than your elbows.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not swing the torso. Raising above shoulder level brings in the trapezius and overloads the neck with osteochondrosis: lift strictly to parallel with the floor.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Light dumbbells are enough. You can do it seated on a chair to remove any body swing.
