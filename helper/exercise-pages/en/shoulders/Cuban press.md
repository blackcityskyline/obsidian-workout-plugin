# Cuban press

**Worst** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Shoulders, rotator cuff

## Step-by-step technique

1. Row the weight up, rotate the forearms upward, then press overhead.

## Key points

- Just a step from the upright row as the worst of the worst: it adds external rotation but from an elevated, internally rotated position.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Overhead pressing after an elevated pull needs a stable lower back; with hyperlordosis skip it.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Skip it. Use the Arnold press or the dumbbell scoop press.
