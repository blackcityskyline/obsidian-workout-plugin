# Rear delt rows

**Best** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Rear delts

## Starting position

Hinge forward fully until your torso is parallel to the floor.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Raise the dumbbells out to the sides, leading with your pinkies and driving the movement with your rear delts.

## Key points

- Don't squeeze your shoulder blades together too hard, so the tension doesn't shift to your back.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting your torso up out of the hinge.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Hold the hinge with a flat back and a long neck, torso parallel to the floor; keep the weight light and do not use momentum. Rest the forehead on a chair if the back tires.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Support the forehead on a chair or sofa arm; light dumbbells.
