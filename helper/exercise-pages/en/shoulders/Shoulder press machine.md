# Shoulder press machine

**Better still** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Shoulders

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit and press the handles overhead.

## Key points

- Limited because you are seated; the neutral grip options are valuable for those who need to press overhead that way.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not dig the back into the pad and arch to press.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do a seated dumbbell press with a neutral grip on a high-back chair.
