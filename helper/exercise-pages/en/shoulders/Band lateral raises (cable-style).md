# Band lateral raises (cable-style)

**Best** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Side delts

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Step on the band with the opposite foot. Grab the end of the resistance band.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Raise your arm out to the side. The band provides maximum resistance at the peak of the movement.

## Key points

- Lets you maintain constant tension.

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging your shoulder (traps) up.
