# Abduction rows

**Better still** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Side delts

## Starting position

Hinge forward to a 45° torso angle. Let the dumbbells hang in your hands.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull the dumbbells up, flaring your elbows wide out to the sides at a 90° angle to your torso.

## Key points

- Emphasizes the rear delts and rhomboids.

## Common mistakes

- Pinning your elbows to your body.
