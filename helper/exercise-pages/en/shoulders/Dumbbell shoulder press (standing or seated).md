# Dumbbell shoulder press (standing or seated)

**Target muscles:** Front and side delts, triceps.

## Starting position

Sit on a straight-backed chair (to protect the lower back) or stand upright. Dumbbells at the shoulders, palms facing forward or neutral.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, press the dumbbells up along an arc.
2. On the inhale, lower the dumbbells to the shoulders slowly (3 seconds).

## Key points

- Brace your abs and squeeze your glutes so you don't arch your lower back.

## Common mistakes

- Flaring your elbows straight out to the sides (pathological shoulder impingement).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The seated version with back support is preferable with hyperlordosis because it prevents leaning back during the press.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A chair with a high back gives support. Press your lower back into the backrest and do not lean back.
