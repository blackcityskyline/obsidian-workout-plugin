# Behind-the-neck press

**Worst** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Shoulders, triceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press a bar overhead from behind the neck.

## Key points

- It takes the shoulders out of the scapular plane and forces an awkward elbow position. Overhead presses are great, but not from this position.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

The bar behind the head forces the neck forward and arches the lower back. A clear no with your neck and spine.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Skip it. Use a seated dumbbell press with a high-back chair.
