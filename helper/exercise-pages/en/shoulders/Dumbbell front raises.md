# Dumbbell front raises

**Best** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Front delts

## Starting position

Stand with the dumbbells resting against the front of your thighs.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Raise the dumbbell in front of you to eye/shoulder level.
2. Lower it smoothly. Alternate arms or raise both together.

## Key points

- Isolates the front delt.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning your torso back as you lift.
