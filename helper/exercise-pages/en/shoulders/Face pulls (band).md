# Face pulls (band)

**Better still** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Rear delts

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Anchor the band at chest or eye level.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull the band toward your eyes/forehead, actively flaring your elbows out to the sides and rotating your hands back.

## Key points

- Keeps your shoulder joints healthy and works the rear delts.

## Common mistakes

- Pulling to your stomach or chin.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the ribs down and the neck neutral; do not lean back, and pull toward the eyes, not up toward the ceiling.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Anchor the band at face height on a door; a loop band works the same.
