# Hip huggers

**Almost best** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Rear delts

## Starting position

Stand upright holding heavy dumbbells.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull the dumbbells back along the line of your hips, driving your elbows as far back as possible.

## Key points

- Works the rear delts from a standing position.

## Common mistakes

- Bending your torso forward.
