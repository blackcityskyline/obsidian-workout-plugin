# Lateral raise machine

**Worst** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Side delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit in the machine and press the pads out to the sides.

## Key points

- The fixed hand placement does not allow external rotation of the shoulder, which can cause more pain than it fixes with impingement.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do dumbbell or band lateral raises with the thumbs up.
