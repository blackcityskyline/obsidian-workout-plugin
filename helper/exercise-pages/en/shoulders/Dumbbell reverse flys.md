# Dumbbell reverse flys

**Better** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Rear delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hinge forward and raise the dumbbells out to the sides.

## Key points

- The most popular rear delt exercise, but you cannot extend the arm far enough behind the body, so it is inferior to rear delt rows.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Brace the abs and support the head on the back of a chair to protect the neck and back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Lie chest-down on the sofa and do it with light dumbbells.
