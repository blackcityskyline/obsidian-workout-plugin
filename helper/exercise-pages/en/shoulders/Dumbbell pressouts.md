# Dumbbell pressouts

**Better** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Front delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hold a dumbbell or plate at the chest and press it straight out in front.

## Key points

- A good front delt builder, but it lacks the range of motion of other front delt exercises.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Ribs down and glutes tight so the lower back does not arch as the weight moves forward.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use one dumbbell held in both hands, or a plate.
