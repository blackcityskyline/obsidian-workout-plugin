# Upright rows

**Worst** · [Shoulder Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZafawk3arc)

**Target muscles:** Shoulders, traps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull a bar or dumbbells up the body to the chin with the elbows leading.

## Key points

- A terrible exercise biomechanically: it puts the shoulder joint in impingement and should not be part of a shoulder routine long term.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Pulling to the chin crams the shoulder and pushes the chin forward. Do dumbbell high pulls instead.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Skip it. Use dumbbell high pulls or rear delt rows.
