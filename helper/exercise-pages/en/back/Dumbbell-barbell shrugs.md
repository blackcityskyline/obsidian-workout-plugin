# Dumbbell/barbell shrugs

**Almost best** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius

> [!warning] Note: this exercise can put a high axial load on the neck and spine. With cervical issues use a light weight or skip it.

## Starting position

Stand tall with the barbell in front of you or dumbbells at your sides. Keep your back straight and eyes forward.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and deliberately raise your shoulders straight up.
2. Hold the tension in your traps for 1–2 seconds.
3. Lower the weight slowly.

## Key points

- No rotating movements at the shoulder joints (this injures the rotator cuff).

## Common mistakes

- Rolling your shoulders in circles.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Heavy weight on the traps loads the neck and spine axially. Keep the neck neutral, raise the shoulders straight up, no rolling and no head jutting; use moderate weight.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells up to 16 kg are enough; hold with the elbows soft and pause at the top.
