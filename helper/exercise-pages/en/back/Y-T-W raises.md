# Y-T-W raises

**Target muscles:** Lower and middle trapezius, rhomboids, neck and scapular stabilizers.

## Starting position

Lie face down on a mat/sofa. Face looks straight at the floor, neck relaxed.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Y phase: raise the arms at 45° (thumbs up), squeeze the shoulder blades together, hold for 2 sec.
2. T phase: spread the arms straight out to the sides, hold for 2 sec.
3. W phase: bend the elbows to the sides, raise the forearms, squeeze the shoulder blades together as much as possible for 2 sec.

## Key points

- Move smoothly and without jerking, driving the motion through deliberate contraction of the mid/lower traps and rhomboids.

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging your shoulders toward your ears (overworking the upper traps).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Completely safe with cervical osteochondrosis and scoliosis. Done without added weight.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A mat on the floor or a sofa works. For neck comfort, put a rolled towel under your forehead.
