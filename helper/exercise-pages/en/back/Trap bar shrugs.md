# Trap bar shrugs

**Best** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius

> [!warning] Requires a trap (hex) bar.

> [!warning] Note: this exercise can put a high axial load on the neck and spine. With cervical issues use a light weight or skip it.

## Starting position

Step inside the trap bar and grab the handles with a neutral grip.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Stand up tall. Exhale and raise your shoulders straight up.
2. Hold the peak contraction. Lower the bar.

## Key points

- The neutral grip takes unnecessary stress off the shoulder joints.

## Common mistakes

- Tilting your head forward.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The trap bar puts a high axial load on the neck and spine and invites jutting the head forward. Keep it light, neck neutral, shoulders straight up.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No trap bar: use two dumbbells of up to 16 kg at your sides.
