# Angled shrugs (band)

**Almost best** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Anchor the band low in front of you. Grab the ends with your arms extended forward and up at about 45°.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull your shoulder blades and shoulders back and up along the diagonal line of the band's resistance.
2. Return to the starting position.

## Key points

- Targets the mid traps.

## Common mistakes

- Bending your arms at the elbows.
