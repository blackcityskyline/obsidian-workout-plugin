# Straight-arm pushdowns (band)

**Better still** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Back (lats and mid-back)

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Anchor a resistance band or loop band at a high point (door anchor, pull-up bar). Grab the ends of the band and step back. Lean your torso forward 20–30° with your knees slightly bent. Your arms are extended up and forward, with a minimal bend in the elbows.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and, without bending your arms at the elbows, pull the band down in an arc toward your thighs.
2. At the bottom, squeeze your shoulder blades together as hard as you can and contract your lats.
3. Inhale and return your arms to the starting position under control.

## Key points

- Your arms work as rigid levers; there is no bending or extending at the elbow joint.

## Common mistakes

- Bending your arms and turning the exercise into a triceps pushdown.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the ribs down and the glutes tight; do not arch to finish the pull and do not lock the neck forward.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Anchor the band high at a door or on the pull-up bar.
