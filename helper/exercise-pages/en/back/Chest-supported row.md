# Chest-supported row

**Best** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, mid-back

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench or a sofa edge and row two dumbbells to the sides of the ribs.

## Key points

- Ranked with the barbell row as best: it allows overload while keeping the lower back safe, and it is the alternative when the low back is an issue.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The support takes the load off the lower back, which makes it the safest heavy row for your spine.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Lie chest-down on the sofa edge with the neck neutral and row two dumbbells.
