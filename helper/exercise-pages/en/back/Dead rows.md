# Dead rows

**Almost best** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Back (lats and mid-back)

## Starting position

The barbell or dumbbells rest on the floor. Set your feet shoulder-width apart. Lower into a deep hinge with a straight back (like the bottom position of a deadlift).

## Step-by-step technique

1. Every rep starts from a dead stop (the weight rests on the floor).
2. Exhale and pull the weight toward your waist with an explosive but controlled movement.
3. Lock in your shoulder blades, then lower the weight back to the floor.
4. Pause on the floor before the next rep.

## Key points

- Removes constant tension from the lower back between reps and builds explosive strength.

## Common mistakes

- Yanking the weight off the floor with a rounded back.
