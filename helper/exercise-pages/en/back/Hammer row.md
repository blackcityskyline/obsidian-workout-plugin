# Hammer row

**Better** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Lats, mid-back

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull the handles back on a plate-loaded row machine.

## Key points

- Depends on the machine: with a range of motion from high to low you get a good lat stretch and the chest does not cave around the pad.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do chest-supported dumbbell rows.
