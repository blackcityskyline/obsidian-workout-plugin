# Bird-Dog

**Target muscles:** Deep spinal extensors, core, glutes.

## Starting position

Get on all fours (on a mat). Palms directly under the shoulders, knees under the hips. Spine neutral.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Simultaneously extend the right arm forward and the left leg back until they form one line with the torso.
2. Hold the top position for 2 seconds, keeping balance and bracing the core.
3. Return to the start and repeat with the left arm and right leg.

## Key points

- Your hips should not sag or rotate. Your lower back stays completely still.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back as you lift the leg.
- Lifting the leg or arm too high, above the line of your body.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The gold standard of back rehabilitation. Eliminates rotation and arching of the lower back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Only a mat is needed. If your knees hurt, put a folded towel under them.
