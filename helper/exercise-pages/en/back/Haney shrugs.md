# Haney shrugs

**Better still** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius

> [!warning] Note: this exercise can put a high axial load on the neck and spine. With cervical issues use a light weight or skip it.

## Starting position

Stand tall, holding a barbell behind your back at glute level with an overhand grip.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and raise your shoulders up and slightly back, bending your elbows slightly at the same time so the bar slides along your glutes and lower back.
2. Hold at the peak.
3. Inhale and return to the starting position.

## Key points

- Isolates the rear portion of the traps.

## Common mistakes

- Using too much weight, which keeps you from lifting your shoulders.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Holding a bar behind the back with heavy shrugs loads the neck and spine axially and pulls the shoulders back. Light weight only; no arching, no rolling the shoulders.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

The 20 kg bar held behind the glutes, or dumbbells held behind the back; use a wall behind you as a cue.
