# Renegade row

**Better** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, core

## Step-by-step technique

1. From a push-up position on two dumbbells, row one dumbbell to the hip while the other arm holds you up.

## Key points

- A metabolic movement with two limits: it needs core stability, so the weights stay light, and it gives little stretch on the lats.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Twisting hips under load tires the lower back. Keep the feet wide and the pelvis level, and use light dumbbells.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells up to 16 kg on the floor.
