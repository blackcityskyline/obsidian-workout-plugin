# Humbler (chest-supported row machine)

**Better still** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Lats, mid-back

## Step-by-step technique

1. Rest the chest on the pad and row the handles.

## Key points

- Gives you a proud chest as you pull the weight, something not offered by the hammer row.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do the sofa chest-supported dumbbell row.
