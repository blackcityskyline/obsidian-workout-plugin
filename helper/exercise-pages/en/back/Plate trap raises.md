# Plate trap raises

**Better still** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius

> [!warning] Note: this exercise can put a high axial load on the neck and spine. With cervical issues use a light weight or skip it.

## Starting position

Stand tall, holding a heavy plate in both hands in front of you by its edges.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and raise your shoulders straight up, trying to touch your ears with them.
2. Pause at the top for 1–2 seconds.
3. Inhale and lower your shoulders. Your arms do not bend at the elbows.

## Key points

- The movement goes strictly straight up and down.

## Common mistakes

- Bending your arms at the elbows (bringing in the biceps).
