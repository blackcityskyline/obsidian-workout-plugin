# Bent-over barbell row

**Best** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, trapezius, mid-back.

## Starting position

Hold a 20 kg barbell with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulders. Lean the torso forward about 45°, knees slightly bent, chest open.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, pull the bar to the lower abdomen / navel, driving the elbows back and up.
2. At the end point, squeeze the shoulder blades hard and hold for 1–2 seconds.
3. On the inhale, lower the bar slowly.

## Key points

- The pull comes from your elbows, not your biceps. Your forearms act merely as hooks.

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum (swinging the torso).
- Pulling the bar to your chest instead of your abdomen (this brings in the upper traps and neck).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the natural arch in the lower back. When fatigued or if the lower back hurts, reduce the torso angle or switch to a supported dumbbell row.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Just the 20 kg bar: no extra plates are needed at first. When tired, replace this with the dumbbell row supported on the sofa.
