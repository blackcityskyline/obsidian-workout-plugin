# 1-arm dumbbell row

**Worst** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, mid-back

## Step-by-step technique

1. Support one hand and one knee on a bench and pull the dumbbell to the hip.

## Key points

- Ranked worst: asymmetrical loading of the lower body can cause a hernia, and the host has had two from it.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

The one-sided load twists the pelvis and strains the lower back and groin. The tripod row keeps the loading symmetrical and is the safer version.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do tripod rows or two-dumbbell chest-supported rows on the sofa instead.
