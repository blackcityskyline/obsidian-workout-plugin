# Seated row (band)

**Better still** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Back (lats and mid-back)

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Sit on the floor with your legs extended (or slightly bent). Loop the band around your feet and grab its ends. Keep your back upright, chest forward, and shoulders down.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and pull the band toward your lower abdomen.
2. Keep your elbows as close to your torso as possible, focusing on squeezing your shoulder blades together.
3. Hold the point of peak contraction for 1 second.
4. Inhale and return smoothly to the starting position.

## Key points

- Keep good posture and do not lean back excessively as you pull.

## Common mistakes

- Shrugging your shoulders toward your ears.
- Rounding your upper back.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Sit tall on the floor with the back long; do not round the back at the start or lean far back at the end. Sit with the back against the sofa if you cannot keep it straight.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Loop the band around the feet; sit with the back against the sofa for support.
