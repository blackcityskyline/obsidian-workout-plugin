# Trap pulldown

**Better** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Upper traps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull a handle down in front while shrugging into an isometric contraction of the upper traps.

## Key points

- Looks like a behind-the-neck pulldown but is anything but: the isometric upper-trap contraction gives a unique growth stimulus.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the head neutral and do not pull behind it; with your neck, use a band and light weight.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Band anchored high.
