# Dumbbell high pulls

**Better still** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius, rear delts

> [!warning] Note: this exercise can put a high axial load on the neck and spine. With cervical issues use a light weight or skip it.

> [!warning] Explosive movement: keep it controlled and light, no jerking, especially with spine or neck issues.

## Starting position

Stand with the dumbbells hanging in your lowered hands in front of your thighs.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and, with a powerful burst (you may dip your knees slightly), pull the dumbbells up along your body.
2. Your elbows should stay above the level of your hands at all times and travel out to the sides and up.
3. Lower back to the starting position.

## Key points

- Works the traps and the mid/rear delts.

## Common mistakes

- Raising your hands higher than your elbows.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Explosive jerking with weight: high axial load on the neck and spine and a risk of jerking the neck. With cervical issues, use very light weight and a controlled pull, or skip it.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Safer swap: slow upright rows with a band under the feet, elbows leading, no jump.
