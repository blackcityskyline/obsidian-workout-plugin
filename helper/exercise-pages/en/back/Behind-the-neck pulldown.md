# Behind-the-neck pulldown

**Worst** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, upper back

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull a bar down behind the head to the base of the neck.

## Key points

- Ranked worst for safety: it takes the shoulders out of the scapular plane, their healthiest position.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The bar behind the head pushes the neck forward and rotates the shoulders. With cervical osteochondrosis this is a clear no.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do band lat pulldowns to the front instead.
