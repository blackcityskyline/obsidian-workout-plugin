# Tripod row

**Better still** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps.

## Starting position

Stand beside the sofa or a sturdy chair with your feet on the floor in a split stance (one foot slightly ahead). Rest one hand on the seat/edge for support and hinge forward until your torso is almost parallel to the floor, back flat. The other hand holds a dumbbell hanging straight down.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, row the dumbbell in an arc toward the hip (the "pocket"), keeping the elbow close to the body and the torso still.
2. At the top, squeeze the shoulder blade toward the spine and hold for 1–2 seconds.
3. On the inhale, lower the dumbbell slowly (3 seconds) until the lat is fully stretched.

## Key points

- Keep your torso static; do not rotate your shoulders.

## Common mistakes

- Flaring the elbow out to the side instead of driving it toward the hip.
- Twisting your torso as you lift a heavy weight.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Both feet on the floor plus the supporting hand give three points of contact, which unloads the lower back. Keep the spine neutral: no twisting of the torso and no jerking. The neck continues the spine, eyes on the floor.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use the edge of the sofa or the seat of a stable chair pushed against a wall as the support. Do the same number of sets on both sides.
