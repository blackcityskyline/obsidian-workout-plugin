# Urlacher

**Better** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Traps, rear delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hold dumbbells with the elbows bent and raise them up and out with external rotation.

## Key points

- Keeps hitting the traps with lighter weights, which limits overload, but adds external rotation and works without a cable.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Light dumbbells.
