# Above-the-knee rack pulls

**Worst** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Traps, upper back

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull a bar from pins set above the knees to lockout.

## Key points

- A great assistance exercise, but doing it above the knee leads to problems a below-the-knee version avoids.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Heavy axial load on the spine; skip the heavy bar. Below-the-knee pulls with light weight are the better version.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No pins or heavy bar at home: do dumbbell shrugs.
