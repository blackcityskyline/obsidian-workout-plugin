# Lat pulldowns (band)

**Almost best** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Back (lats and mid-back)

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

The band is anchored high above your head. Kneel or sit on a chair. Hold the band with a wide grip. Keep your chest up and shoulder blades depressed.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and pull the band to your upper chest.
2. Drive your elbows down and slightly back.
3. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the bottom.
4. Inhale and slowly let the resistance pull your arms back up.

## Key points

- Do not pull the band behind your head; the pull goes strictly to your chest.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning your torso back more than 20°.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the torso upright and the ribs down; do not lean back to pull the band, and do not crane the neck forward.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Anchor the band to the pull-up bar or the top of a door.
