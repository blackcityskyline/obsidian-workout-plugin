# Incline Y raises

**Better** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Lower and middle traps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench and raise the arms overhead into a Y with the thumbs up.

## Key points

- A great corrective exercise that hits the lower and middle traps, but it cannot be loaded as intensely as other trap exercises.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Lie chest-down on the sofa with light dumbbells or a band.
