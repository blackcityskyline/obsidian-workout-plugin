# Face pulls with overhead extension (band)

**Almost best** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Set the band at face level. Hold the ends with a neutral or overhand grip.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull the band toward your nose/forehead, flaring your elbows out to the sides (Face Pull).
2. From that position, without lowering your elbows, raise your hands overhead (external rotation and press).
3. Slowly return to the starting position.

## Key points

- Excellent for strengthening the rotator cuff and upper back.

## Common mistakes

- Arching the lower back as you raise your arms.
