# Meadows row (landmine)

**Almost best** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, rhomboids, rear delts

> [!warning] Requires a barbell anchored in a corner (landmine setup).

## Starting position

Place one end of the barbell in a landmine (or in a corner). Stand perpendicular to the bar. Take a staggered stance: the leg closest to the bar is set back. Hinge forward and brace your forearm of the front-leg side on your thigh. Grab the end of the bar with a pronated grip (palm facing back).

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and pull the end of the bar up, driving your elbow high out to the side and back.
2. Emphasis is on the rear delt, rhomboids, and upper lats.
3. Inhale and lower under control.

## Key points

- Your elbow rises above the level of your back.

## Common mistakes

- Helping with the whole body (swinging).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Support the free hand on a chair or the sofa, and keep the back neutral; do not twist the torso to lift the bar higher.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Wedge one end of the 20 kg bar in a corner with a towel and put plates on the other end.
