# Pendlay row

**Better** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, mid-back

## Step-by-step technique

1. Bend over with the bar on the floor, row it to the lower chest, and return it to the floor each rep.

## Key points

- It gives overload and easy access to a barbell, but without mobility and form the low back rounds, which holds its rank down.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Starting every rep from a dead stop invites a rounded lower back. Use a chest-supported row instead.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

The 20 kg bar works, but the sofa chest-supported dumbbell row is safer.
