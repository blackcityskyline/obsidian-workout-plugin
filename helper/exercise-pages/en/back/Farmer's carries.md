# Farmer's carries

**Better still** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius

> [!warning] Note: this exercise can put a high axial load on the neck and spine. With cervical issues use a light weight or skip it.

## Starting position

Stand between two heavy dumbbells or kettlebells. Squat down with proper form and a straight back and grab the weights. Stand up tall: chest up, shoulders pulled back and down, abs rock solid.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Start moving forward with short, controlled steps.
2. Keep your torso perfectly upright and do not sway from side to side.

## Key points

- Builds grip, core, and upper trap strength.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the shoulders and bending the back.
