# Rolling shrugs

**Worst** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Traps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Roll the shoulders in a circle while holding weight.

## Key points

- Might surprise some as the way shrugs were always taught, but the safety of the shoulders matters, so it stays in the worst group.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do straight up-and-down shrugs with dumbbells instead.
