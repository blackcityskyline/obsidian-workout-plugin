# Deadlift

**Better** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Back, glutes, hamstrings

## Step-by-step technique

1. Stand with the bar over the mid-foot, hinge down with a flat back, and stand up by driving through the floor.

## Key points

- Considered by some the king of exercises, but it is not the best for lat size: little pure lat hypertrophy.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Heavy axial and lumbar load is exactly what scoliosis and hyperlordosis rule out. Skip heavy pulls; the Romanian deadlift with a light load is the compromise.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No heavy bar at home: do Romanian deadlifts with the 20 kg bar or dumbbells.
