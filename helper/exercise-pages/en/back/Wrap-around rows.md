# Wrap-around rows

**Almost best** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius

## Starting position

The band is anchored to the side at waist or chest height. Stand sideways to the anchor point and grab the band with the hand farthest from it.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and pull the band, wrapping your arm around your torso toward the opposite side.
2. This creates a deep peak contraction in the lat.
3. Return to the starting position, controlling the tension.

## Key points

- The range of motion goes past the midline of your body.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the hips excessively.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Rotate through the upper back only; keep the hips square and do not twist the lower back for extra range.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Anchor the band to a door at waist height and stand sideways.
