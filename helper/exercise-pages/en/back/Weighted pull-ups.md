# Weighted pull-ups

**Best** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Lats, biceps, mid-back

> [!warning] Requires a pull-up bar or parallel bars (dip bars).

## Starting position

Attach a weight (plate or kettlebell) to a dip belt. Grab the pull-up bar with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder-width. Hang with straight arms and your core braced.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and start the movement by depressing your shoulder blades, then pull yourself up using your back.
2. Pull until your chin is above the bar and your chest approaches it.
3. Inhale and lower yourself under control until you reach a full hang with your back fully stretched.

## Key points

- Avoid jerking and kicking your legs (kipping).

## Common mistakes

- Incomplete range of motion at the bottom or at the top.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Start by pulling the shoulder blades down; do not swing, kick or arch the lower back, and keep the chin tucked slightly rather than jutting out. Use a modest added load.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Needs a bar and a belt or a backpack with a load. Without a bar, do band lat pulldowns.
