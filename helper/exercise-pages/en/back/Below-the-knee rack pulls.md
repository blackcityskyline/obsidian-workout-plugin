# Below-the-knee rack pulls

**Better still** · [Trap Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7OSC-RfKOI)

**Target muscles:** Trapezius, lower back, glutes

> [!warning] Requires a power rack or stands to set the bar below the knee.

> [!warning] Note: this exercise can put a high axial load on the neck and spine. With cervical issues use a light weight or skip it.

## Starting position

The barbell is set on pins or safety arms of the rack at just below knee level. Step up close and grab the bar with a shoulder-width grip, keeping your back perfectly straight.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull the bar off the pins with a powerful drive by extending your hips and back, standing up to a fully upright position.
2. Lower the bar back onto the rack.

## Key points

- Lets you work with heavy weights and train the spinal erectors and traps.

## Common mistakes

- Overextending (leaning back) at the top.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Pulling heavy from pins loads the lower back and neck, and a hip and back extension at the top invites a lumbar arch. With scoliosis and hyperlordosis, keep the load light, stop at upright without leaning back, and skip it if the back aches.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Safer swap: Romanian-style hinge with dumbbells or the 20 kg bar from a low chair seat, moving slowly with a flat back.
