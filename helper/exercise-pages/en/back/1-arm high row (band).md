# 1-arm high row (band)

**Almost best** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Back (lats and mid-back)

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Anchor the band overhead. Kneel on one knee (the arm on the same side as the kneeling leg is up) or sit. Extend your arm overhead, holding the band under tension.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and pull your elbow straight down to your side.
2. At the bottom, squeeze your lat and hold the position.
3. Inhale and return up under control, letting your lat stretch.

## Key points

- Focus on the movement of your elbow, not your hand.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning your torso toward the working arm.
