# Twisting push-ups

**Better still** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest, triceps, core

## Starting position

Get into a push-up position.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lower into a push-up. As you press back up, twist your hips and torso, driving one knee toward the opposite elbow.

## Key points

- Combines pec and core work.

## Common mistakes

- Letting your hips sag.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Twisting under load strains the spine in rotation, which is unwelcome with scoliosis. Keep the twist small and slow, brace the core, and drop the twist if the back complains.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do plain push-ups, or add the knee drive without twisting the hips.
