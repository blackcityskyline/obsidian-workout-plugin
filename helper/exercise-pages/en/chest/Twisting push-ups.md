# Twisting push-ups

**Better still** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest, triceps, core

## Starting position

Get into a push-up position.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lower into a push-up. As you press back up, twist your hips and torso, driving one knee toward the opposite elbow.

## Key points

- Combines pec and core work.

## Common mistakes

- Letting your hips sag.
