# Incline press (band, cable-style)

**Almost best** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Chest (upper chest)

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Anchor the band behind you, low. Take a staggered stance or sit.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Perform pressing movements with your arms forward and slightly upward.
2. At the end point, bring your hands together for maximum peak contraction.

## Key points

- The resistance increases as your arms straighten.

## Common mistakes

- Losing tension in the band at the bottom.
