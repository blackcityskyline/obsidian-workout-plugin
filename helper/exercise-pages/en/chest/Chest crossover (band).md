# Chest crossover (band)

**Better still** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

The bands are anchored at shoulder height. Your arms are spread out to the sides and slightly bent.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Bring your arms together in front of your chest in an arc, crossing your hands at the end point to increase the contraction.

## Key points

- Keep the elbow bend angle constant.

## Common mistakes

- Turning the fly into a press.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Hold the ribs down and the torso still; do not lean forward to cross the hands, and keep the elbows slightly bent.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Anchor two bands at shoulder height on a door, or one band through a door loop.
