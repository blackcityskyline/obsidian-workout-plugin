# 30° incline bench press

**Almost best** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest (upper chest)

## Starting position

Set the bench to 30°. Hold the barbell or dumbbells at upper chest level. Keep your shoulder blades squeezed together.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press up through the standard range of motion, exhaling on the effort and lowering the weight under control.

## Key points

- A 30° angle is considered the ideal gold standard for isolating the upper chest.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting your hips off the bench.
