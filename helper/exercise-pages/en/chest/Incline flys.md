# Incline flys

**Worst** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Upper chest

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lie on an incline bench and open the dumbbells out to the sides.

## Key points

- A continued infatuation with the dumbbell fly: biomechanically improper or dangerous for the shoulders.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The deep stretch with the arms wide stresses the shoulder and the back arches. Skip it.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do low-to-high band crossovers instead.
