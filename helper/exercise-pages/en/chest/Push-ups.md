# Push-ups

**Better** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest, triceps, core

## Starting position

Hands slightly wider than the shoulders, body in one straight line.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lower the chest to the floor with the elbows at about 45°, then press back up.

## Key points

- Often too easy to build size, unless done for high reps at the end of a hard chest or push workout.
- Glutes and abs tight, neck neutral.

## Common mistakes

- Sagging hips and a poking chin.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Hips sagging deepens the lumbar arch; keep glutes and abs tight, or do them with hands on the sofa.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Nothing needed. Elevate the feet or add a loaded backpack to make them harder.
