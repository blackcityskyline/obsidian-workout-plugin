# Heavy one-arm crossovers (band)

**Almost best** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Stand sideways to the band and grab it with one hand.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Bring your arm across your whole body, as far as possible past the midline of your torso.

## Key points

- Maximum range of contraction for one side of the chest.

## Common mistakes

- Turning your entire torso to follow your arm.
