# Guillotine press

**Worst** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Chest, upper chest

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press a bar down to the neck instead of the chest.

## Key points

- In the worst group of upper chest exercises: biomechanically improper or just dangerous for the shoulders.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The bar travels over the neck and face; with the cervical issues this is a clear no.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Skip it: use the 30° incline press.
