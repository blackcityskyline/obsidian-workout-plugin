# Incline dumbbell press

**Best** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Chest (upper chest)

## Starting position

Set the bench to an angle of 30–45°. Lie back, squeeze your shoulder blades together, and plant your feet on the floor. Hold the dumbbells at your upper chest.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and press the dumbbells straight up.
2. Inhale and lower them under control until you feel a stretch in your upper chest.

## Key points

- Emphasis is on the upper (clavicular) portion of the pecs.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the bench angle too high (above 45° the load shifts to the delts).
