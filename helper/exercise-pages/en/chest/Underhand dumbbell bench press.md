# Underhand dumbbell bench press

**Almost best** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Chest (upper chest)

## Starting position

Lie on a flat bench and hold the dumbbells with your palms facing you (supinated grip).

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press the dumbbells up, keeping your elbows closer to your torso.
2. Lower them until they lightly touch your thighs/chest.

## Key points

- Increases upper chest activation even on a flat bench.

## Common mistakes

- Dumbbells slipping out because of the unfamiliar grip (use a moderate weight).
