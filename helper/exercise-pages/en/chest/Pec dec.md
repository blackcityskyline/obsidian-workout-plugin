# Pec dec

**Better still** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Chest, rear delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit and bring the handles together in front of the chest, or reverse for the rear delts.

## Key points

- Allows both chest and rear delt training, but getting into the machine wrongly can cause shoulder issues.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a band crossover.
