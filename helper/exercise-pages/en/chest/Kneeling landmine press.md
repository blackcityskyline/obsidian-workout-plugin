# Kneeling landmine press

**Better** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Chest, front delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Kneel and press the end of an anchored bar up and forward with one hand.

## Key points

- Gives those with shoulder pressing pain an option that does not hurt.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Kneeling takes the legs out and keeps the ribs down; press without arching.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Wedge the end of the 20 kg bar into a corner with a towel.
