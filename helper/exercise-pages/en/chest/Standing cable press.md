# Standing cable press

**Worst** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest, triceps, core

## Starting position

Stand facing away from the cable or band anchor, handles at chest height.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press the handles forward until the arms are straight, then return under control.

## Key points

- Ranked worst: the weak link is the core, which gives out before the chest does.
- Staggered stance, ribs down.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning back against the pull.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Leaning back against the pull turns it into a lower-back arch. Staggered stance, glutes and abs tight.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A band anchored behind you in a door. A better choice is the lying band press.
