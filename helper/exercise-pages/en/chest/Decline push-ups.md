# Decline push-ups

**Better** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Upper chest, front delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Put the feet on the sofa and do a push-up.

## Key points

- A great bodyweight chest exercise, ultimately limited by its inability to produce top-end strength.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the glutes and abs tight so the hips do not sag.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Feet on the sofa edge.
