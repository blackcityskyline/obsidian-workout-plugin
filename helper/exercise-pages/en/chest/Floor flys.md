# Floor flys

**Better** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest

## Starting position

Lie on the floor, dumbbells over the chest, elbows slightly bent.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Open the arms until the upper arms touch the floor, then squeeze back up.

## Key points

- Better than the bench version since the floor limits the stretch, but still limited by the resistance it gives in adduction. Cables or bands are better.
- Keep the elbow angle fixed.

## Common mistakes

- Slamming the arms on the floor.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The floor stops the stretch and supports the back; keep the feet planted and no arch.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells up to 16 kg on the floor or the sofa edge.
