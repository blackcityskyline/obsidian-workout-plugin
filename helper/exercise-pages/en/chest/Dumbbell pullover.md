# Dumbbell pullover

**Better still** · [Back Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4vU3tzVM70)

**Target muscles:** Intercostals, serratus, lats, lower pecs.

## Starting position

Lie with your upper back across the sofa/bench. Hold one dumbbell with both hands by the upper plate above your chest, arms slightly bent.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the inhale, smoothly take the dumbbell behind your head in an arc until the chest is fully stretched.
2. Hold for 1–2 seconds at the bottom.
3. On the exhale, use the chest and lats to bring the dumbbell back to the start.

## Key points

- The angle at your elbows stays unchanged throughout the entire set.

## Common mistakes

- Bending and extending your arms at the elbows (turning it into a triceps exercise).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Expands the rib cage and relieves compression of the thoracic spine.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

The sofa supports the upper back. Keep the hips lower than the shoulders and do not arch the lower back when taking the dumbbell behind the head.
