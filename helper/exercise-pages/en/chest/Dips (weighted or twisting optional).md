# Dips (weighted or twisting optional)

**Almost best** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest, triceps, front delts

> [!warning] Requires a pull-up bar or parallel bars (dip bars).

## Starting position

Support yourself on the dip bars with straight arms.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lean your torso forward at about 30° and flare your elbows out slightly.
2. Lower until you feel a comfortable stretch in your chest (roughly a 90° angle at the elbows).
3. Press yourself back up.

## Key points

- The more you lean forward, the more your chest works (rather than your triceps).

## Common mistakes

- Sinking too deep at the bottom (risk of shoulder injury).
