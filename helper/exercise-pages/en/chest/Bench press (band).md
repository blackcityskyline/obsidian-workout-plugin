# Bench press (band)

**Better still** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest, triceps, front delts

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Run the band across your upper back and hold its ends in your palms, with or without dumbbells. Lie on a bench or on the floor.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Perform a classic press. The band adds resistance in the top portion of the movement.

## Key points

- Lets you keep working the pecs in the fully contracted phase.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the band slide up onto your neck.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the lower back in a natural curve, feet planted, shoulder blades down; do not arch to chase the band's pull.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Run the band across the upper back and lie on the sofa or the floor; a floor press protects the shoulders.
