# Bench flys

**Worst** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Chest, front delts

## Starting position

Lie on a bench, dumbbells over the chest, palms facing each other.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lower the dumbbells out to the sides in a wide arc with slightly bent elbows, then bring them back up.

## Key points

- Ranked last: the stretched position puts the shoulder joint at risk, and there are much better, safer chest options.
- Elbows slightly bent, wrists stiff.

## Common mistakes

- Going too deep and too heavy, which strains the shoulder.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

The deep stretch with the arms out wide stresses the shoulder, and a lifted chest arches the lower back. Skip it; if you keep it, go light and stop when the arms are level with the chest.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do floor flys or band crossovers instead.
