# Dumbbell bench press

**Best** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Pectoralis major, front delts, triceps.

## Starting position

Lie on a bench or on the floor (floor press). Take the dumbbells and bend the elbows at 45° to the torso (do not flare the elbows to 90°).

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, press the dumbbells up powerfully, bringing them together over the center of the chest.
2. On the inhale, lower the dumbbells under control over 3 seconds.
3. Pause for 1 second at the bottom, feeling the stretch in the chest muscles.

## Key points

- Your elbows should not flare out to the sides at 90° (a risk to the shoulder joints).

## Common mistakes

- Letting your shoulder blades come off the bench as you press.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

45° elbows protect the shoulder joints. Do not over-arch the lower back into a powerlifter-style bridge.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Without a bench, press from the floor (floor press): the floor limits the range of motion and protects the shoulders. Bend the knees so the lower back stays on the floor.
