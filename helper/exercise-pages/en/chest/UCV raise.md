# UCV raise

**Better still** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Chest (upper chest)

## Starting position

Stand tall with a dumbbell in one hand at your hip, palm facing up.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and raise your arm from low to high, across your body on a diagonal, to the level of the opposite shoulder.
2. Squeeze your upper chest. Lower back to the starting position.

## Key points

- The movement is driven by bringing your arm toward the midline.

## Common mistakes

- Doing the work by curling with your biceps.
