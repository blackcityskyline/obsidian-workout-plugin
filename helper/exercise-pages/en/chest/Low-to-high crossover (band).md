# Low-to-high crossover (band)

**Almost best** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Chest (upper chest)

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

The bands are anchored low behind you. Stand in the center with your arms down at your sides and pulled back.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and bring your arms together in front of you in an arc from low to high, up to chin level.
2. Squeeze your pecs at the top.

## Key points

- Isolates the upper chest.

## Common mistakes

- Bending your arms at the elbows.
