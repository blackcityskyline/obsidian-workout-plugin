# Cavaliere crossover

**Better** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Upper chest

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull the cable handles from low to high in front of the chest.

## Key points

- Limited by the range of motion, but it closely mimics the fiber direction of the upper clavicular fibers.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do it with a band anchored low.
