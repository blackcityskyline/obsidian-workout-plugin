# Incline dumbbell squeeze press

**Better still** · [Upper Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqFfh5IZUBs)

**Target muscles:** Chest (upper chest)

## Starting position

Set an incline bench (30–45°). Hold the dumbbells in front of your chest so they are pressed tightly against each other.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Squeeze the dumbbells into each other with maximum force the whole time.
2. Exhale and press them up without breaking contact.
3. Inhale and lower them back down.

## Key points

- Creates massive isometric tension in the inner chest.

## Common mistakes

- Letting up on the pressure between the dumbbells.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Squeeze the dumbbells together with the lower back in its natural curve; do not bridge. The constant tension keeps the shoulders stable.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A sofa back or cushions for the incline; one pair of dumbbells.
