# 60 degree incline bench press

**Worst** · [Chest Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykQlXirszbo)

**Target muscles:** Upper chest, front delts

## Starting position

Set the bench to about 60° and lie back with the bar or dumbbells at the chest.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press up and back to full extension, then lower to the upper chest.

## Key points

- Ranked worst: too steep an angle moves most of the work to the front deltoid instead of the chest. At 30° the same press is a superior choice.
- If you want an incline, lower the bench to about 30°.

## Common mistakes

- Setting the bench too steep.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

A steep incline is close to an overhead press and pushes the lower back into an arch. Use 30° instead.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use dumbbells with the sofa back or a cushioned incline at about 30°.
