# Dumbbell reverse sprinter lunge

**Better** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Glutes, quads

## Step-by-step technique

1. Step back into a lunge with a forward lean, then drive up with the front leg.

## Key points

- Mimics the chest and thigh position at the bottom of a leg press but resists hip extension far better on the way up.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the back flat in the forward lean.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells up to 16 kg.
