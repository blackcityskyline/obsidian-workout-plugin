# Drop squat

**Better** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Drop quickly into the bottom of a squat and stand up.

## Key points

- Great for learning proper squat mechanics, but limited in the ability to load the movement.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Bodyweight or a light dumbbell at the chest.
