# Dumbbell Spanish squat / TKE split squat

**Better still** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads

## Step-by-step technique

1. Anchor a band behind the knees, sit back and squat upright against its pull.

## Key points

- The perfect replacement for the leg extension that lets assistance muscles work in concert.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A band around a pole or a door.
