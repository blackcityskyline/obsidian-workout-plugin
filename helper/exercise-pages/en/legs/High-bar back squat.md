# High-bar back squat

**Almost best** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Squat with the bar high on the traps, torso upright.

## Key points

- The king of leg exercises: allows significant overload, and believe it or not you can target the quads better with it.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

A bar on the shoulders is an axial load, which with scoliosis and hyperlordosis you avoid. Use goblet or reverse-lunge variations.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use goblet squats with dumbbells.
