# Sled push / pull

**Better still** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Push or pull a loaded sled across the floor.

## Key points

- An excellent option for athletes who want bigger quads without an eccentric focus, but it needs a sled and room.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No sled at home: do a heavy towel drag on a smooth floor or step-ups.
