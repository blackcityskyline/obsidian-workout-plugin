# Good girl / bad girl machine

**Worst** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Glute medius

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit and press the knees apart or together against the pads.

## Key points

- Often used to build the glute medius, which is better done as shown in the video.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do side-lying leg raises or a band abduction.
