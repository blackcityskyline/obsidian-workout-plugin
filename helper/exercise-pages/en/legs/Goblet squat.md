# Goblet squat

**Better** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hold a dumbbell at the chest and squat down between the knees.

## Key points

- Great for a more metabolic pursuit of hypertrophy, but the load is limited by the weight available and by thoracic mobility.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The weight in front keeps the torso upright, which is easy on the lower back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A dumbbell up to 16 kg.
