# Leg press (feet low)

**Better still** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press the platform away with the feet placed low, going through the full range.

## Key points

- Lets you use heavy weight, but you must use the full range of motion. With the feet high it was ranked worst for glutes, since the range is poor and the overload far from optimal.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not let the lower back round off the seat at the bottom.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No machine: use reverse lunges or step-ups.
