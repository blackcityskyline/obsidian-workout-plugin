# Prone hamstring curl

**Worst** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Hamstrings

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lie face down on a machine and curl the heels toward the glutes.

## Key points

- Lowest spot: it can build bigger hamstrings but often brings about low back pain.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Hip flexors pull the pelvis into the pad and arch the lower back: with hyperlordosis, avoid. Use a seated or standing curl.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do Romanian deadlifts or a band hamstring curl.
