# Sissy squat

**Worst** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, knee tendons

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lean back and bend the knees forward, rising onto the toes.

## Key points

- One of the better exercises to strengthen knee tendons with proper progression, but weak for building quads: little overload.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the ribs down and do not arch the lower back as you lean.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Hold a doorframe for balance.
