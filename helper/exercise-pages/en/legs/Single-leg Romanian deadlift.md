# Single-leg Romanian deadlift

**Better** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Glutes, hamstrings, deep pelvic stabilizers.

## Starting position

Hold one dumbbell in the hand opposite to the standing leg. Stand on one leg with the other slightly lifted.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lean the torso forward while extending the free leg back in one line with the torso.
2. Lower the dumbbell to knee level / the upper shin of the standing leg.
3. Return to the start using the glute of the standing leg.

## Key points

- Make sure your hips do not rotate toward the raised leg. Both hip bones point straight down at the floor.

## Common mistakes

- Rotating the hips outward.
- Losing balance because the tempo is too fast.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

A great movement for correcting pelvic asymmetry in scoliosis. Do not let the pelvis rotate sideways while bending.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

If balance is hard, lightly touch a chair back or the wall with your free hand.
