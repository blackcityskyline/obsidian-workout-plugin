# Lying hamstring curl

**Worst** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Hamstrings

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lie face down in the machine and curl the pad.

## Key points

- Not a good choice because of lower back pain; with other options available it sits in the worst category.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

The knees and thighs are driven into the pad and the hip flexors overwork, arching the lower back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use the standing or seated curl, or a band curl.
