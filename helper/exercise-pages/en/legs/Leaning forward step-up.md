# Leaning forward step-up

**Almost best** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Step up with the torso leaning forward over the working leg.

## Key points

- Capable of delivering stronger, bigger glutes and worth a spot in your glute workout.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the back flat in the lean.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A chair or the sofa edge, dumbbells at the sides.
