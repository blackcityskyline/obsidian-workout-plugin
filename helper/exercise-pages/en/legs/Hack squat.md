# Hack squat

**Almost best** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Squat in the hack machine, shoulders against the pad.

## Key points

- Takes the Smith machine squat and improves it by letting the body move naturally within a fixed path.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Heavy weight loads the lumbar spine; keep it moderate and the back pressed to the pad.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No machine: use goblet squats and reverse lunges.
