# Barbell hip thrust

**Best** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit with the upper back on a bench, roll the bar over the hips and drive them up.

## Key points

- The best glute exercise of the best of the best: the mechanics and the ability to check all three criteria.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

At the top, tuck the pelvis into a posterior tilt and do not hyperextend the lower back. Use a moderate load.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Back on the sofa edge with a dumbbell or the 20 kg bar on the hips.
