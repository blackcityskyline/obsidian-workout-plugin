# Dumbbell / barbell reverse lunge

**Almost best** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Step back into a lunge and push through the front leg to return.

## Key points

- Loved for the ability to overload and for how it feels for someone with knee problems.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Hold dumbbells at the sides, not a bar on the back, so the spine is not loaded.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells up to 16 kg.
