# Smith machine squat

**Worst** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Squat with the bar on a fixed vertical or angled rail.

## Key points

- Probably the worst version of a squat: the fixed path does not allow your own biomechanics.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Heavy bar on the back with a forced path loads the lumbar spine. Skip.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No Smith machine at home: use goblet squats.
