# Glute bridge

**Target muscles:** Gluteus maximus, hamstrings.

## Starting position

Lie on your back on a mat, knees bent, feet flat on the floor shoulder-width apart.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, squeeze the glutes and push the hips up until knees, hips and shoulders form a straight line.
2. Hold the top position for 2 seconds, squeezing the glutes as hard as you can.
3. On the inhale, lower the hips under control, hovering just above the floor.

## Key points

- Keep your chin tucked to your chest and your eyes looking straight ahead, not at the ceiling, to avoid hyperextending the lower back.

## Common mistakes

- Overextending the lower back at the top.
- Placing your feet too far from your hips (the load shifts to the hamstrings) or too close (the quads take over).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No axial load at all. Avoid excessive arching of the lower back when lifting the hips (the hips work, not the lumbar spine).

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Done on the floor. To make it harder, place a dumbbell or plate on your hips and hold it with your hands.
