# Standing hamstring curl

**Better** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Hamstrings

## Step-by-step technique

1. Stand and curl one leg against the pad.

## Key points

- Better than the lying curl: you are on your feet and the knees and thighs no longer drive into the pad, so the hip flexors do not overwork.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a band around the ankle.
