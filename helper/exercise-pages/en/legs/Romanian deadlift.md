# Romanian deadlift

**Almost best** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Hamstrings, glutes, spinal extensors.

## Starting position

Stand upright and hold a 20 kg barbell or dumbbells with an overhand grip. Feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent ("soft") with the angle locked.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Start the movement strictly by pushing the hips back, sliding the weight down along your thighs. Lowering takes 3 seconds.
2. Lower the weight to mid-shin, feeling a strong stretch in the hamstrings. Pause for 1 second.
3. On the exhale, squeeze the glutes, drive the hips forward and return to the start.

## Key points

- This is a hip hinge movement, not a squat. Your knees do not bend any further as you lower the weight.

## Common mistakes

- Rounding the upper or lower back.
- Squatting too deep (turning the deadlift into a squat).
- Letting the weight drift away from your legs.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Rounding the lower or upper back is strictly forbidden. Keep the spine one rigid straight line. Do not lift the head (look forward and down).

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Two dumbbells work instead of a barbell. Stand sideways to a mirror or film yourself on your phone to check that your back stays straight.
