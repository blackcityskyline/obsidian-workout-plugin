# Bulgarian split squats

**Almost best** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quadriceps, glutes, hamstrings.

## Starting position

Stand with your back to the sofa/bench (about 60–90 cm away). Place the toe of your rear foot on it. Hold dumbbells at your sides. Keep your torso balanced and chest open.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the inhale, lower smoothly by bending the front leg. The lowering (eccentric) phase lasts a strict 3 seconds.
2. Go down until the thigh is parallel to the floor (or until the rear knee almost touches the floor).
3. On the exhale, drive up powerfully through the heel of the front foot and return to the start.

## Key points

- Your front knee should track strictly in line with your second toe and not cave inward.

## Common mistakes

- Taking too short a step (excessive stress on the front of the knee).
- Shifting your weight onto the back leg.
- Fully locking out the working knee with a snap.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep a slight forward lean of the torso (10–15°) to take stress off the lower back with hyperlordosis and to emphasize the glutes. Do not let the knee cave inward.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use the sofa or a chair seat as support for the rear foot. If the chair slides, push it against a wall.
