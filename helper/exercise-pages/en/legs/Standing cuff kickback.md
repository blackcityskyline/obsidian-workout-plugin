# Standing cuff kickback

**Better** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Attach a cuff to the ankle and kick the leg back against the resistance.

## Key points

- Improves the isolated kickback by adding weight resistance.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not arch the lower back to swing the leg higher.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A band around the ankle anchored low.
