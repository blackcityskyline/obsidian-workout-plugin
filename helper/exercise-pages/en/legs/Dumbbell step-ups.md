# Dumbbell step-ups

**Better still** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hold dumbbells and step up onto a box or chair, then lower slowly.

## Key points

- A great choice for an eccentric focus on the quads, but the weight and your balance are limited.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A sturdy chair or the sofa edge with dumbbells.
