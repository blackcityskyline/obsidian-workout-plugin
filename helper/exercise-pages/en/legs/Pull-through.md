# Pull-through

**Almost best** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Glutes, hamstrings

## Step-by-step technique

1. Face away from a cable, hinge down and drive the hips forward to pull the rope through the legs.

## Key points

- Capable of delivering stronger, bigger glutes.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Hinge at the hip with a flat back; do not round or arch.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a band anchored low behind you.
