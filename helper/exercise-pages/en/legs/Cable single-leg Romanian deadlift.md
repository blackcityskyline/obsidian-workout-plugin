# Cable single-leg Romanian deadlift

**Better still** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Hamstrings, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Stand on one leg holding a cable and hinge at the hip.

## Key points

- A slight improvement over the dumbbell version thanks to the added stability in the frontal plane.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Rounding the back is not allowed; keep a flat spine and hinge only at the hip.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a band under the standing foot.
