# Belt squat

**Best** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hang the weight from a belt at the hips and squat without a load on the spine.

## Key points

- Ranked with the front squat as best and should not be ignored if you have access.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The weight hangs from the hips, so the spine is unloaded: the best squat for your back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Make it with a dumbbell hung from a belt or a backpack held at the hips.
