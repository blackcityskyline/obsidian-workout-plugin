# Low-bar back squat

**Almost best** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Glutes, quads

## Step-by-step technique

1. Squat with the bar low on the rear delts and a forward lean.

## Key points

- The incredibly powerful squat, here recommended in a low-bar position to target the glutes more effectively.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

The low-bar squat leans the torso forward under an axial load, the opposite of what your spine needs. Avoid.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use goblet squats or reverse lunges.
