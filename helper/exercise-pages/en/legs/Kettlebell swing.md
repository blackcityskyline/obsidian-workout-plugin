# Kettlebell swing

**Better still** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Glutes, hamstrings

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hinge at the hips and swing a kettlebell up to chest height by driving the hips.

## Key points

- An amazingly athletic glute exercise that can become more of a conditioning challenge than a glute builder.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

An explosive hinge with a quick snap; the risk is lumbar arching at the top. Keep it low, with the glutes squeezed.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a dumbbell held by one end.
