# Glute-ham raise

**Almost best** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Hamstrings, glutes

## Step-by-step technique

1. Anchor the feet in the GHD and lower the torso, then curl back up with the hamstrings.

## Key points

- An underutilized exercise capable of delivering stronger, bigger glutes and hamstrings.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

At the top the lower back can arch into extension; keep the glutes squeezed and the torso straight.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Anchor the feet under the sofa and do slow Nordic curls.
