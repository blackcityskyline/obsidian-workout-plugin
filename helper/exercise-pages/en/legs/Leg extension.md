# Leg extension

**Better** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit in the machine and extend the knees against the pad.

## Key points

- Isolates the quads directly, but with existing knee issues it could make them worse.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No machine: use Spanish squats with a band.
