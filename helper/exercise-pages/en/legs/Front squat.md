# Front squat

**Best** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads

## Step-by-step technique

1. Rest the bar on the front delts and squat with the torso upright.

## Key points

- No better quad exercise: it overloads well and targets the quads directly.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

The bar on the front keeps the torso upright, but it is still a loaded squat; keep the weight modest and the ribs down.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do goblet squats with a 16 kg dumbbell instead.
