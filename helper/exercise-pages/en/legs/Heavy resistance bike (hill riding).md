# Heavy resistance bike (hill riding)

**Better** · [Quad Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud9hEOxJMpQ)

**Target muscles:** Quads

## Step-by-step technique

1. Ride a bike or stationary bike with the resistance cranked up.

## Key points

- You would be hard pressed to find a serious cyclist without large quads: crank the resistance and stay consistent.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Sit upright and keep the neck neutral.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a bike on hills or a stationary bike with the resistance up.
