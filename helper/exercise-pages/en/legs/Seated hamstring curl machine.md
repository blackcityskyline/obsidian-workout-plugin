# Seated hamstring curl machine

**Better still** · [Glute Exercises Ranked | Hamstrings (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1jp2uhfO8M0)

**Target muscles:** Hamstrings

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit in the machine and curl the pad down with the heels.

## Key points

- A far better machine choice for bigger legs: the direction of force takes the hip flexors out and saves your back.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A band hamstring curl lying on the sofa or a slider curl with towels.
