# Incline dumbbell power bombs

**Almost best** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps (long head)

## Starting position

Sit on a bench set to 75° and hold a dumbbell with both hands behind your head.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Extend your arms up overhead.

## Key points

- The bench back prevents excess arching in your lower back.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the dumbbell touch your neck.
