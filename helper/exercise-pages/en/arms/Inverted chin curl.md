# Inverted chin curl

**Better** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lie under a bar or sturdy table, grip it and curl your body up with the arms.

## Key points

- A solid bodyweight biceps option, but hard to overload; it gets too easy without a weighted vest.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the body in one line and the glutes tight; no sagging at the lower back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a sturdy table edge or a bar in a doorway.
