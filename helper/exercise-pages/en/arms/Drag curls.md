# Drag curls

**Better still** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Starting position

Stand holding a barbell or dumbbells.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Curl the weight, sliding it straight up along your torso (touching your stomach and chest) and driving your elbows back.

## Key points

- Takes tension off the front delt and isolates the biceps.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the weight travel forward away from your body.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the elbows pulled back and the weight close; do not lean back, it turns the movement into a lumbar arch.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells or the 20 kg bar; doing it with the back against a wall keeps the torso still.
