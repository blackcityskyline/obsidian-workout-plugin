# Preacher curl machine

**Worst** · [Gym Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9jvUHbbIhU)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Curl on a seated preacher machine.

## Key points

- Best used standing with a downward sloping pad; seated it invites the mechanics that cause biceps tears.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do standing dumbbell curls.
