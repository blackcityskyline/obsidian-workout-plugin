# JM press

**Better still** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps

## Starting position

Lie on a bench with the bar over your chest.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lower the bar on a diagonal path straight toward your neck/upper chest, tucking your elbows forward and down. From that position, press up.

## Key points

- A hybrid of the close-grip bench press and the skull crusher.

## Common mistakes

- Overextending your wrists.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Lower back stays on the bench, feet planted; no arch. Keep it light: the bar travels close to the neck and face.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use dumbbells with a neutral grip on the sofa or the floor; alone with a bar over your face is risky.
