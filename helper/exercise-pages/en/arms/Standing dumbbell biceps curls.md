# Standing dumbbell biceps curls

**Best** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps brachii.

## Starting position

Stand upright, dumbbells in hands, elbows pinned to your sides.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, bend the arms at the elbows and raise the dumbbells to the shoulders with supination (turning the palm up).
2. Squeeze the biceps for 1 second at the top.
3. On the inhale, lower the weight over 3 seconds.

## Key points

- Keep your elbows still and pinned to your torso.

## Common mistakes

- Letting your elbows drift forward or back.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not swing the torso or lean back: this adds arching in the lower back. If the weight pulls you back, reduce it or do the curl seated on a chair with a backrest.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Any adjustable dumbbells will do. To control your torso, stand with your back against a wall.
