# Incline dumbbell curls

**Almost best** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Starting position

Sit on a bench set to 45° and let your arms hang down freely, holding the dumbbells.

## Step-by-step technique

1. From the stretched position, curl your arms, supinating as you go.

## Key points

- Maximally stretches the long head of the biceps at the bottom.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting your shoulders off the bench.
