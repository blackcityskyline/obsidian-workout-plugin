# Hammer curls

**Target muscles:** Brachialis, brachioradialis, biceps.

## Starting position

Stand upright, hold the dumbbells with a neutral grip (palms facing each other).

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, bend the arm at the elbow and raise the dumbbell to the shoulder without rotating the wrist.
2. Lower under control on the inhale.

## Key points

- Loads the brachioradialis and the brachialis.

## Common mistakes

- Using momentum from your hips.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the torso still and elbows pinned. Do not lean back: arching the lower back is undesirable with hyperlordosis.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

You can do it seated on a straight-backed chair, which eliminates cheating with the torso.
