# Improvised preacher curls

**Better** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Lower biceps and the biceps peak.

## Starting position

Lean over the back of a chair or the edge of the sofa, firmly fixing your triceps and elbows on the support.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, curl the arms, lifting the dumbbells without lifting the elbows off the support.
2. Peak pause of 1–2 seconds at the top.

## Key points

- Completely eliminates any chance of cheating.

## Common mistakes

- Dropping the weight abruptly at the bottom.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not round the back or twist the torso. Make the whole lean from the support, not from the lower back; choose a weight that does not require helping with the torso.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

The support is a chair back or the edge of a sofa. For comfort, put a folded towel under the elbows.
