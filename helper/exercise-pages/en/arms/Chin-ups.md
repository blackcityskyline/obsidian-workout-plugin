# Chin-ups

**Almost best** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps, lats

> [!warning] Requires a pull-up bar or parallel bars (dip bars).

## Starting position

Hang from the bar with an underhand grip (palms facing you), shoulder-width apart.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull yourself up, focusing on bending your elbows.

## Key points

- An excellent compound exercise for the biceps.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging and using an incomplete range of motion.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not arch the lower back or kick the legs for height; pull with the elbows and keep the neck neutral, chin not poking forward.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Needs a bar. Without one: band-assisted rows with an underhand grip, or a towel over a sturdy door.
