# Weighted upright dips

**Almost best** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps, chest

> [!warning] Requires a pull-up bar or parallel bars (dip bars).

## Starting position

Support yourself on the dip bars.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Keep your torso strictly upright, with your legs straight or bent behind you.
2. Lower yourself down, driving your elbows back close to your body.

## Key points

- The upright position puts all the load on the triceps.

## Common mistakes

- Leaning your torso forward (shifts the load to your chest).
