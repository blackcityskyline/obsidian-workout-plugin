# Close-grip bench press

**Almost best** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps, chest

## Starting position

Lie on a bench. Take a shoulder-width grip (or a 20–30 cm gap between your hands).

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lower the bar to your lower chest, keeping your elbows as close to your torso as possible. Press up.

## Key points

- A powerful foundational exercise for all three heads of the triceps.

## Common mistakes

- Gripping too narrow (causes wrist pain).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Press with the shoulder blades pinned down and a natural lower back; no bridge. A narrow grip loads the wrists, keep them straight.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells with a neutral grip on the sofa edge, or press from the floor.
