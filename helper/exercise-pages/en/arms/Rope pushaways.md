# Rope pushaways

**Better** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Stand facing away from a cable, hold a rope overhead and press it forward.

## Key points

- Less effective due to the physics: you want a perpendicular line of force to the forearm, and this move loses it.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use band overhead extensions.
