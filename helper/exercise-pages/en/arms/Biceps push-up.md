# Biceps push-up

**Worst** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Wrists, biceps (barely)

## Step-by-step technique

1. Push-up with the hands turned back so the fingers point toward the feet.

## Key points

- Stays at the bottom for one reason: it does not hit the biceps at all.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The rotated wrists are stressed; there is simply no reason to do it.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Skip it: do chin-ups or dumbbell curls.
