# Flex curls / lip buster curls (band)

**Better still** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Anchor the band at eye level.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Keeping your elbows up and parallel to the floor, curl your hands toward your lips/forehead.

## Key points

- Highly isolates the biceps.

## Common mistakes

- Dropping your elbows.
