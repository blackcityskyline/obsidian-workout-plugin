# Overhead extensions (band)

**Better still** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps (long head)

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Stand with your back to the band's anchor point (low or behind you). Hold your hands bent behind your head.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Extend your arms overhead, forward and up.

## Key points

- Deep stretch on the triceps.

## Common mistakes

- Flaring your elbows out to the sides.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Pressing overhead with a leaning torso loads the lower back. Split stance, ribs down, glutes tight; no arching to push the hands up.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Anchor the band low behind you or stand on it. If the back arches, do it seated on a chair.
