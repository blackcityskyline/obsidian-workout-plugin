# Lying overhead triceps extension (arms back at 45°)

**Target muscles:** Long head of the triceps.

## Starting position

Lie on your back. Extend your arms with the dumbbells back behind your head at a 45° angle and lock the elbows in that position.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the inhale, bend the arms only at the elbows, lowering the dumbbells behind your head.
2. On the exhale, extend the arms back to the starting 45° angle.

## Key points

- Keeps constant tension on the long head of the triceps.

## Common mistakes

- Returning your arms to a strictly vertical position.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not arch the lower back: press it into the floor or bench and bend your knees. Do not take the arms back farther than 45° so as not to overload the shoulders and neck.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Done on the floor or the sofa. Bend the knees and put your feet on a support so the lower back stays against the surface.
