# Reverse curls

**Worst** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Brachioradialis, biceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Curl a bar or dumbbells with an overhand grip.

## Key points

- A great curling movement but not a great biceps exercise: it works the brachioradialis, not the biceps.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A good forearm builder with light dumbbells or a band.
