# Lying crossface extension

**Worst** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lie on a bench and lower one dumbbell across the face to the opposite shoulder.

## Key points

- A single-arm Tate press. The arm position is the starting position of the Hawkins-Kennedy impingement test, so there is too much internal rotation.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use lying triceps extensions instead.
