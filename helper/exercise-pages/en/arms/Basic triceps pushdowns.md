# Basic triceps pushdowns

**Better** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Stand at a high cable and push the handle down until the arms are straight.

## Key points

- Tension is high at the start but declines as you approach the contracted position at the bottom.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a band over a high hook or a door.
