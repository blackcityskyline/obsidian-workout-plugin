# Cable curl

**Better** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Stand at a low cable and curl the handle.

## Key points

- Great top contraction, but tension drops at the bottom because the line of force is in line with the muscle.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use a band anchored low.
