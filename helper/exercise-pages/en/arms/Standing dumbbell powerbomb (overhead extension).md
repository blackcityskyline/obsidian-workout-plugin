# Standing dumbbell powerbomb (overhead extension)

**Worst** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hold one dumbbell overhead in both hands, lower it behind the head and extend.

## Key points

- At the bottom of the list: it puts the shoulder at risk through weighted internal rotation and does not stretch the triceps best.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Overhead standing work arches the lower back. If you do an overhead extension, sit on a chair with a high back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do lying triceps extensions or band overhead extensions.
