# Reverse-grip pushdowns (band)

**Better still** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps (long head)

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Anchor the band up high. Use an underhand grip (palms facing up).

## Step-by-step technique

1. Extend your arms down until your elbows are fully locked out.

## Key points

- Emphasizes the medial head of the triceps.

## Common mistakes

- Letting your elbows come away from your torso.
