# Spider curls

**Better still** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Starting position

Lie chest-down on an incline bench (45°). Let your arms hang straight down vertically, holding a barbell or dumbbells.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Bend your elbows to curl the weight, without letting your upper arm move out of the vertical position.

## Key points

- Peak biceps contraction at the top.

## Common mistakes

- Letting your elbows drift back toward the bench.
