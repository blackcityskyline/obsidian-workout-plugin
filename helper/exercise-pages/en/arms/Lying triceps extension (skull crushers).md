# Lying triceps extension (skull crushers)

**Best** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Lateral and medial heads of the triceps.

## Starting position

Lie on your back, arms with dumbbells extended perpendicular to the floor.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Bend the arms at the elbows toward the ears/forehead, keeping the elbows still.

## Key points

- Keep your elbows locked in place and don't let them flare out.

## Common mistakes

- Moving through the entire shoulder joint.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Lower back pressed to the floor or bench, knees bent. Lower the dumbbells slowly and under control without swinging the elbows.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Done on the floor or the sofa. Lower the dumbbells to the sides of your head, not exactly onto the forehead.
