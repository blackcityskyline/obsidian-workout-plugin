# Triceps kickbacks

**Almost best** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps

## Starting position

Hinge over with your torso parallel to the floor. Pin your elbow to your side and keep it raised.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and extend your arm back until it forms a fully straight line.

## Key points

- Your elbow doesn't drop during the movement.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging the weight instead of extending under control.
