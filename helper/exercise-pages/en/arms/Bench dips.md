# Bench dips

**Worst** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps, front delts

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hands behind you on a bench, lower the body by bending the elbows and press up.

## Key points

- Ranked worst; the host points to a dedicated video on his channel about it.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

The shoulders are pulled behind the body under load; skip them.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do weighted upright dips or close-grip push-ups instead.
