# Concentration curls

**Worst** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit with the elbow braced on the inner thigh and curl the dumbbell.

## Key points

- Ranked worst: a non-athletic curl where you are completely seated and off your feet.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Seated and hunched, which loads the neck and upper back; stand instead.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do standing alternating dumbbell curls instead.
