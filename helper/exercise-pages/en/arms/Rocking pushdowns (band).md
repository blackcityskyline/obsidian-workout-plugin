# Rocking pushdowns (band)

**Almost best** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps

> [!warning] In the original this exercise uses a cable machine; here it is done with a resistance band.

## Starting position

Stand facing the band anchored high, like a cable stack.

## Step-by-step technique

1. As you extend your arms, lean your torso slightly forward, and on the return, shift your hips back, intensifying the tension at the peak.

## Key points

- A dynamic adjustment of the biomechanics.

## Common mistakes

- Overly chaotic movements.
