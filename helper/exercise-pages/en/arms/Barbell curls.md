# Barbell curls

**Almost best** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Starting position

Stand with an underhand grip, shoulder-width apart.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Curl the bar to the level of your upper chest, then lower it under control.

## Key points

- The classic foundational exercise for building biceps mass.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging your torso (cheating).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the ribs down and the glutes lightly tight; do not lean back to swing the bar up, that is a lumbar arch.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use the 20 kg bar or dumbbells. Stand with your back against a wall to take the swing out.
