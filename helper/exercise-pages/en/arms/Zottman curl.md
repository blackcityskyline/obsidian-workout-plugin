# Zottman curl

**Better** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps, forearms

## Step-by-step technique

1. Curl the dumbbells with a supinated grip, rotate to overhand at the top, and lower them slowly.

## Key points

- Biceps do their elbow flexion here, but pronating the hands moves the eccentric load to the brachioradialis, limiting the biceps overload.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

No specific spine concerns: keep the ribs down, a neutral neck and a steady back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Dumbbells up to 16 kg.
