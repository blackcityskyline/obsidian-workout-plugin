# Cobra push-ups

**Better still** · [Triceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZrAiIvpZvhM)

**Target muscles:** Triceps (long head)

## Starting position

Lie face-down and place your hands under your chest, close to your body.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Press your upper body up by straightening your elbows, keeping your hips on the floor.

## Key points

- Isolated bodyweight triceps work.

## Common mistakes

- Using your lower back muscles.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Lumbar hyperextension: with hyperlordosis the lower back is already arched and this movement deepens it. Skip it, or lift only a few centimetres.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A safer swap: plain push-ups or incline push-ups with hands on the sofa. If you keep it, glutes and abs stay tight and the ribs stay on the floor.
