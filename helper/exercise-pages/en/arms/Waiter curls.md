# Waiter curls

**Better still** · [Biceps Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6g57DJntRXk)

**Target muscles:** Biceps

## Starting position

Hold one dumbbell vertically with both palms cupped under the top plate (like a tray).

## Step-by-step technique

1. Curl the dumbbell up to your chin, keeping your palms parallel to the ceiling.

## Key points

- Loads the long head of the biceps.

## Common mistakes

- Clamping the dumbbell with your fingers.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Hold the torso still and the ribs down; do not lean back as the dumbbell rises, and keep the neck relaxed.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

One dumbbell, held by the top plate. Stand with your back against a wall.
