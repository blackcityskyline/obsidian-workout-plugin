# Single-sided slow carry (suitcase carry)

**Better still** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Obliques, deep core, grip

## Starting position

Pick up a heavy dumbbell/kettlebell in one hand.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Walk slowly in a straight line, resisting the lean of your torso toward the weight.

## Key points

- Powerful work for the obliques as stabilizers.

## Common mistakes

- Letting your shoulder girdle tilt.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Stand tall and resist the lean toward the weight; ribs down, neck long. Keep the weight moderate, because heavy one-sided load is hard on a scoliotic back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

One dumbbell of up to 16 kg and a hallway; switch hands each lap.
