# Ab wheel rollout

**Better** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Abs, core

## Step-by-step technique

1. Kneel and roll the wheel forward, then pull back with the abs.

## Key points

- A good movement with major limitations that make it less globally successful for everyone.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Rolling out far arches the lower back into extension, a clear risk with hyperlordosis. Keep a posterior pelvic tilt and short range.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No wheel: use a towel on a smooth floor.
