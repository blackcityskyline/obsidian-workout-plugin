# Lying leg raise

**Worst** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Lower abs, hip flexors

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lie on the back and raise straight legs up and down.

## Key points

- Adds unnecessary stress to the low back by overworking the hip flexors rather than the abs.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

The lower back arches off the floor as the legs lower, which is bad with hyperlordosis. Do the sliding tuck instead.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Skip it; do the sliding tuck on a towel.
