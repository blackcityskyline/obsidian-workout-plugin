# Hanging leg raise

**Better** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Lower abs, hip flexors

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hang from a bar and raise straight legs to the horizontal.

## Key points

- A good movement with major limits: if grip strength is weaker than the abs, you come down before the abs fatigue.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Straight legs swing the pelvis and arch the lower back. Use the hanging knee raise instead.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Needs a bar: use the sliding tuck instead.
