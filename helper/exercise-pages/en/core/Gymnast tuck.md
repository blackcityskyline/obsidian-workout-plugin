# Gymnast tuck

**Almost best** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Lower abs, hip flexors

## Starting position

Support yourself on parallettes, dip bars or the floor with your arms straight.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Pull your knees up to your chest and hold that position (or perform it dynamically).

## Key points

- Strong static-dynamic loading of the abs.

## Common mistakes

- Swinging.
