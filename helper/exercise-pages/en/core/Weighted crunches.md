# Weighted crunches

**Target muscles:** Rectus abdominis.

## Starting position

Lie on your back, knees bent, a 10 kg weight plate pressed to the chest.

## Step-by-step technique

1. On the exhale, crunch the upper torso, lifting only the shoulder blades.
2. The lower back stays firmly pressed to the floor through the whole movement.

## Key points

- It's a curl of the spine, not a lift of the whole straight back.

## Common mistakes

- Lifting your lower back off the floor.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not pull on your neck with your hands and do not tuck the chin to the chest: the abs work, not the neck. Keep the range small, just the shoulder blades off the floor.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Instead of a plate, take a dumbbell and press it to your chest with both hands. Do it on a mat.
