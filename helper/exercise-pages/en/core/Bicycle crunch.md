# Bicycle crunch

**Worst** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Abs, obliques

## Step-by-step technique

1. Alternate bringing opposite elbow and knee together while lying on the back.

## Key points

- Regarded as one of the best, but most people do it wrong: no rotation at the shoulders, so it turns a good move into a commonly misdone one.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the lower back pressed to the floor and the neck relaxed.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No equipment.
