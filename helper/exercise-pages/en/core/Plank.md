# Plank

**Worst** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Abs, core

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hold a straight line on the forearms and toes.

## Key points

- One of the least favorite ab exercises: too easy and remedial for almost everyone, so you need a version hard enough to fail within 90 seconds.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Do not let the hips sag into the lower back.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

No equipment: use a harder variation such as the sliding tuck.
