# Sliding tuck (towel on the floor)

**Best** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Lower abs, hip flexors

## Starting position

Get into a push-up position with your feet on a towel/sliders on a smooth floor.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Without hopping, pull your feet toward your hands by curling your abs and sliding.

## Key points

- A controlled movement with no momentum.

## Common mistakes

- Arching your lower back in the plank position.
