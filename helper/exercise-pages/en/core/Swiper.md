# Swiper

**Almost best** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Abs and obliques

## Starting position

Lie on your back with your knees bent and your shoulder blades lifted.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Alternate reaching your right hand to your right heel, then your left hand to your left heel, along the sides.

## Key points

- Continuous tension on the rectus abdominis and obliques.

## Common mistakes

- Resting your neck on the floor between reps.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the lower back pressed to the floor and the neck relaxed; reach along the sides without twisting the lower back hard.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Floor only; a rolled towel under the head.
