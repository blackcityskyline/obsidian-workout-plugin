# Hanging corkscrew

**Better still** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Abs and obliques

> [!warning] Requires a pull-up bar or parallel bars (dip bars).

## Starting position

Hang from a pull-up bar.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Raise your knees while rotating your pelvis to the right and left in alternation.

## Key points

- Engages the obliques.

## Common mistakes

- Losing control of your shoulder girdle.
