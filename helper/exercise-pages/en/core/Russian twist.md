# Russian twist

**Worst** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Obliques, abs

## Step-by-step technique

1. Sit leaning back and rotate the torso side to side.

## Key points

- The host likes it, but it is the most commonly misperformed ab exercise: people tap the hands side to side without rotating.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Twisting loads the spine in rotation, which is unwelcome with scoliosis. Skip or do it very small and slow.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Use the side bridge twist instead.
