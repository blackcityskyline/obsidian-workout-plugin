# Dumbbell side bend

**Worst** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Obliques, quadratus lumborum

## Step-by-step technique

1. Hold a dumbbell in one hand and bend sideways.

## Key points

- Adds unnecessary stress to the low back by overworking the quadratus lumborum instead of the abs.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Side-bending under load stresses the lumbar spine and with scoliosis is a clear no.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Do the single-sided slow carry instead.
