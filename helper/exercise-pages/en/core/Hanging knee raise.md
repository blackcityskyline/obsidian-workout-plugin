# Hanging knee raise

**Better still** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Abs, hip flexors

> [!warning] Requires a pull-up bar or parallel bars (dip bars).

## Starting position

Hang from a pull-up bar.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and pull your knees up to your chest, making sure to curl your pelvis up.

## Key points

- If your pelvis doesn't curl up, only your hip flexors are working, not your abs.

## Common mistakes

- Raising your legs by swinging.
