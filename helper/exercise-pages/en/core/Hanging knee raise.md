# Hanging knee raise

**Better still** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Abs, hip flexors

> [!warning] Requires a pull-up bar or parallel bars (dip bars).

## Starting position

Hang from a pull-up bar.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Exhale and pull your knees up to your chest, making sure to curl your pelvis up.

## Key points

- If your pelvis doesn't curl up, only your hip flexors are working, not your abs.

## Common mistakes

- Raising your legs by swinging.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

> [!warning] Spine caution: this exercise can load the lower back or neck more than most. Read the safety note and go light.

Hanging can pull on the lumbar spine and neck. Curl the pelvis up (posterior tilt) rather than just lifting the legs, no swinging; the lower back must not arch when you lower.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Needs a bar. Lying alternative: lying knee-ups with the lower back on the floor.
