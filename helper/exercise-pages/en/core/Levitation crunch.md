# Levitation crunch

**Almost best** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Abs (rectus abdominis)

## Starting position

Lie on your back with your arms extended overhead and your legs raised slightly.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Lift your shoulder blades and legs at the same time, keeping your core as tightly braced as possible.

## Key points

- Keep your lower back pressed firmly into the floor.

## Common mistakes

- Arching your lower back (dangerous for the spine).

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the lower back pressed to the floor; if it arches, raise the legs higher or bend the knees. Keep the neck relaxed and do not pull on it.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

Floor only; a rolled towel under the head keeps the neck comfortable.
