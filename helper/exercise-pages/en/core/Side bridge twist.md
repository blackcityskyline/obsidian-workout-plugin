# Side bridge twist

**Almost best** · [Ab Exercises Ranked (BEST TO WORST!)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqXwI1MWZks)

**Target muscles:** Obliques

## Starting position

Set up in a side plank on your forearm.

## Step-by-step technique

1. Thread your top arm under your torso, rotating your chest toward the floor, then return.

## Key points

- Keep your pelvis stable during the rotation.

## Common mistakes

- Letting your hips sag down.

## 🛡️ Spine safety

Keep the body in one line and the hips lifted; do not sag the lower back, and rotate the chest slowly without twisting the pelvis. Shorten the range on the forearm if the shoulder tires.

## 🛋️ At home, with what you have

A floor and a towel under the elbow; knees on the floor for an easier version.
